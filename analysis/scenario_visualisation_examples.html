Visualising 13 scenarios × 5-year profit projections

Six alternatives to a cluttered table or a 13-line chart, using hypothetical data. Self-contained file: all images are embedded.

Heatmap

All 65 values in one compact grid, rows sorted by Year-5 profit. Annotated cells mean it doubles as the table. The strongest single exhibit.

Small multiples

One mini line chart per scenario with the baseline repeated in grey. Shared axes keep trajectories directly comparable.

Spaghetti with highlighting

All 13 lines in light grey; only the headline scenarios in colour with direct labels. Shows the range of outcomes without a legend.

Fan chart

Scenarios collapsed into min–max and interquartile bands around the median. Cleanest option for an executive summary.

Slope chart

Year 1 vs Year 5 only, one line per scenario. Best when intermediate years don't matter; low-growth scenarios in red.

Dot plot

Year-5 profit ranked as lollipops. Best when the endpoint is the decision-relevant number.
