ACNC Charity Register — data.gov.au
Who each charity is: legal name, size, registration date, financial year end.
65,430 registered charities
ACNC Annual Information Statements — data.gov.au
What each charity reports: donations & bequests, total gross income, per year.
6 filing years (FY2019–24), ~51–54k charities each
ABR bulk extract — data.gov.au (XML)
When each charity became DGR endorsed: the day-level endorsed-from date — the event that drives the study.
27,623 register charities carry an endorsement date
DGR Listing — ABN Lookup (ATO)
DGR item numbers — identifies Item 2 ancillary funds (PAF/PuAF) so pass-through vehicles can be excluded.
~26k endorsed entities + ~8.7k funds
ABS CPI 6401.0 — deflator
Financial-year average CPI (2019 = 100) converts nominal donations to real.

charity_master

charity_financials_panel

Endorsement dates are rounded to each charity’s own financial year (non-June year ends handled), so “before” and “after” line up with what the charity actually reported.

65,430 registered charities
27,623 DGR endorsed (ABR date)
691 switchers: endorsed FY2020–23 with valid donations data before and after
646 established switchers — the study cohort (start-ups & ancillary funds excluded)
Each charity’s donations are compared before vs after its own endorsement year → median uplift +47% nominal / +30% real → applied to the ~17,900 charities that would gain DGR under the PC reform ($716m donation base → ~$215m central uplift).
All sources are open government data, refreshed 2026: ACNC Register & AIS datasets (data.gov.au), ABR bulk extract (data.gov.au), ABN Lookup DGR Listing (abr.business.gov.au), ABS CPI cat. 6401.0. Cross-checked against ATO Taxation Statistics (gift deductions; ancillary fund counts within ~5% of ATO anchors). Known limitations: AIS financials are self-reported; ancillary funds not on the DGR Listing are flagged by name-match (“ancillary”) as a documented fallback. Pipeline & calculation: github — unlock-dgr-analysis (R/targets ingest, analysis/dgr_event_study/uplift_calculation.ipynb).
