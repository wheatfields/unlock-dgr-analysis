Event study — what endorsement does
646 established charities that gained DGR FY2020–23, each its own control: median donations +47% nominal / +30% real. Staggered cohorts rule out macro shocks; placebo cohorts ~0%.
Reform scenario impact — who gains & loses
Uplift applied to 17,903 gainers ($716m donation base): +$215m/yr central (+$337m high). PC removals net −$90m — 2,924 losers are donation-heavy (median $62k vs $4.7k).
Grant-pool access — the ancillary-fund channel
$1,527m/yr PAF/PuAF distributions: newly eligible cohorts could access $108–638m depending on allocation rule; ~$170m displaced from incumbents under PC removals.

All sources are open government data (refreshed 2026); no confidential or donor-level records used. Known limitations, stated up front: AIS financials are self-reported; endorsement dates reflect current DGR status (revoked endorsements not visible); ancillary funds missing from the DGR Listing are flagged by name-match as a documented fallback; reform outcome mapping reflects the PC recommendation interpretation agreed with the team (ancillary funds retain DGR).
