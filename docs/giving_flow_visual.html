Australians gave $14.8 billion to charities in 2023-24 — but only DGR charities can tap every channel

Two channels carry the money: direct giving arrives the year it is given; structured giving is parked in ancillary funds and paid out over time — and may only go to DGR-endorsed charities.
Sources & caveats. Total received: ACNC Australian Charities Report (12th ed.), $14.8bn donations & bequests 2023-24; prior year's $18.9bn was inflated by a single $4.9bn gift. Ancillary flows/assets: ATO Taxation Statistics 2023-24, charities Table 4. Individual deduction claims: ATO individuals Table 1, 2021-22 (latest un-skewed year; 2022-23 was $9.1bn on mega-gifts). DGR/non-DGR split applies the 77.9% DGR share of donations computed from ACNC AIS 2024 microdata ($9.17bn of $11.77bn across 49,914 charities) to the ACNC headline; direct = residual after ancillary distributions. Donor-side claims ($4.7bn) and charity-side receipts ($14.8bn) are different measurement sides and years — gifts into ancillary funds are claimed on the way in but reach charities later — so channel widths are indicative, not audited flows. Ancillary distributions are legally restricted to DGR Item 1 charities.
